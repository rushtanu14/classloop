# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ClassLoop serves teachers reviewing class records, students completing approved follow-ups, and individual users organizing their own meeting minutes. They often use the product between classes or meetings, so every home surface must make the next useful action obvious within a glance.

## Product Purpose

ClassLoop turns transcripts, notes, rosters, and resource links into teacher-reviewed recaps, tasks, resources, and student follow-ups. Success means less time reconstructing what happened, clearer follow-through, and no student-facing output before a teacher approves it.

## Brand Personality

Calm, practical, caring. ClassLoop should feel focused enough for a busy school day, trustworthy enough for classroom records, and warm enough that students understand what to do next without pressure.

## Anti-references

- Card-heavy SaaS dashboards that repeat the same number or action in multiple places.
- Setup instructions, incomplete integrations, internal environment variables, or developer status exposed to normal users.
- Decorative AI gradients, excessive pills, generic feature grids, and motion that delays common tasks.
- Dense student views that repeat assignments or turn support signals into public rankings.

## Design Principles

- Lead with one next action. Secondary tools stay available through progressive disclosure or their dedicated route.
- Show the workflow visually. Images and data visuals must explain real ClassLoop behavior, not decorate empty space.
- Keep teacher review authority visible. Students see only approved content and never internal setup state.
- Match the role. Teacher, student, and individual dashboards share a system but prioritize different work.
- Tell the truth in every state. Loading, empty, error, demo, free, and connected states must state exactly what is and is not ready.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Keep keyboard navigation, visible focus, 24px minimum web targets, text alternatives for meaningful images, non-color status labels, responsive reflow, and reduced-motion support. Classroom and student views must remain readable at increased text size and on phone-width screens.
