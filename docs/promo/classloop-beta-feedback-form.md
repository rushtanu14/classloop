# ClassLoop Beta Feedback Form

Last updated: 2026-06-01

Use this as the structure for a Google Form, Typeform, Airtable form, or manual interview sheet. It is designed for sample or anonymized class records first.

## Form Intro

ClassLoop turns transcripts, notes, rosters, and links into teacher-reviewed student follow-ups. This beta test checks whether the generated recap, tasks, resources, and student previews would actually save time after class.

Please use sample or anonymized data unless you are in a reviewed pilot. Do not paste raw student records, grades, passwords, or private student notes into this form.

## Required Fields

1. Role
   - Teacher
   - Tutor
   - Club lead
   - Workshop facilitator
   - Student/other

2. Session type
   - General class
   - Math review
   - CS workshop
   - Tutoring session
   - Club/study group
   - Other

3. What input did you test?
   - Transcript
   - Notes
   - Roster
   - Resource links
   - Sample demo only

4. Did you reach publish preview?
   - Yes
   - No
   - Not sure

5. Recap accuracy rating
   - 1 = not usable
   - 2 = mostly wrong
   - 3 = needs editing but useful
   - 4 = mostly right
   - 5 = would use after minor review

6. Task/action accuracy rating
   - 1 = not usable
   - 2 = mostly wrong
   - 3 = needs editing but useful
   - 4 = mostly right
   - 5 = would use after minor review

7. Student usefulness rating
   - 1 = students would ignore this
   - 2 = unclear
   - 3 = somewhat helpful
   - 4 = useful
   - 5 = very useful

8. Trust rating
   - 1 = would not trust it
   - 2 = major concern
   - 3 = only with careful editing
   - 4 = likely after review
   - 5 = would trust the workflow

## Short Answer Questions

1. What was the most useful generated part?
2. What was wrong, confusing, or missing?
3. What did you have to edit before students could see it?
4. What would make you use this every week?
5. What would stop you or your school from using it?

## Support-Burden Notes

For observed tests, Rushil/Codex should record:

- Where the tester got stuck.
- What had to be explained.
- Whether the issue was product copy, UI layout, import quality, install/setup, account/auth, privacy concern, or missing feature.
- Whether the tester asked to try a second session.

## Pass Signal

A beta test is promising if the tester:

- Reaches publish preview.
- Understands what students would see.
- Can identify what stayed private to the teacher.
- Rates recap/tasks/student usefulness at 3 or higher.
- Says the workflow would save time after editing.

## Hard Stop Signal

Pause and fix before inviting more testers if:

- The tester cannot tell demo/sample data from real/durable data.
- Roster matching or generated tasks create trust-breaking false positives.
- Privacy or school-data boundaries are unclear.
- The tester cannot find review/publish controls.
- The app implies automatic sending/posting before teacher approval.
